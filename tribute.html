FOUR YEARS / ONE INFLUENCE

for Bro Benny

For the one
who saw it
before I did.

A visual thank-you to Bro Benny — mentor, teacher and the person who kept pushing when design still felt like something I could not do.

MENTOR PORTRAIT Bro Benny PHOTO TO BE ADDED

01 / THE PUSH

WHEN I WANTED TO STOP

“You are meant
to do it.”

At the beginning, graphics felt difficult and unnatural. I did not understand it, and I stepped away. Benny did not let the hesitation become the ending.

Sometimes mentorship is simply someone refusing to let you quit too early.

02 / THE METHOD

OBSERVE / COPY / UNDERSTAND

He trained
the eye.

I watched. I copied. I recreated. I learned to look at what another designer had done and ask why it worked. The repetition built a visual instinct long before I had language for it.

WATCHRECREATEREVIEWREPEAT

03 / THE MOMENT

Then one day,
he said it was good.

NO CHANGESGOOD.APPROVED

That tiny moment carried the weight of years of correction. It felt like graduation.

04 / THE LEGACY

WHAT REMAINED

Three years of shaping.
A fourth year of proof.

By the fourth year, the lessons had spread beyond graphics into motion, web design, photography and creative direction. The influence was visible enough that people who had never seen the training process called me a “mini Benny.”
