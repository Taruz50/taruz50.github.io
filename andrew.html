WHERE IT BEGAN

The long way
to here.

Born in Harare in 2001, raised by two loving parents alongside two sisters, then carried into a new chapter in the UK in 2021. The creative story did not begin with certainty. It began with curiosity, resistance, repetition and someone who kept insisting there was more in me.

HARARE / HOME

Before the work,

I was born and raised in Harare, Zimbabwe. Family came first: two loving parents, two sisters, and a foundation that would follow me long after the scenery changed.

A new place.

02

THE RELUCTANT DESIGNER

I did not
like graphics.

It felt hard. I did not understand why things worked, why they looked wrong, or how people made ideas feel effortless. I stopped. Bro Benny kept pushing.

“You are meant to do it.”

At the time, I was hesitant. Looking back, I think he could see something I could not yet see myself.

03

UNDER BENNY

Training the eye
before the style.

I watched how he worked. I was given assignments to recreate what I saw and learn why it worked. Then came Pinterest, YouTube, photo manipulation and a whole new visual vocabulary.

I became drawn to scene-building: small worlds, staged compositions, lighting, depth, characters and objects arranged to feel like something was happening inside the frame.

THE EARLY WORK WAS NOT ALWAYS RECEIVED WELL.

Too much happening.

FROM THE ARCHIVE / 2022

Before the polish,
there was practice.

These are not here because they are perfect. They are here because they are evidence — real pieces from the period where I was learning hierarchy, colour, composition, imagery and how much information a design could carry.

Not hidden. Not rewritten. Part of the journey.

04

THE MOMENT IT CLICKED

No corrections.
No changes.

One day Bro Benny looked at something I had made and simply said it was good. After years of correction, that tiny moment felt enormous.

It felt like I had graduated.

Soon after, he trusted me to run the graphics team.

APPROVEDGOOD.NO CHANGES
05

BECOMING TARUZ

One discipline
was never enough.

Graphics opened into video editing. Video opened into web design. Photography kept pulling at my attention until I was taking pictures almost everywhere I went.

When I moved from campus church into the wider church, people who had never seen the training process called me a “mini Benny.” His influence was visible, but by then I was also beginning to find my own language.

DESIGNMOTIONWEBPHOTOGRAPHY
NOW

PASSING IT ON

The fourth year
became the proof.

Bro Benny shaped me for three solid years. By the fourth, everything he had been building into me began showing up across design, motion, websites, photography and creative direction.

Now I want to share that process. Workshops. Teaching. Spaces where people can experiment, struggle, learn and eventually realise that creativity is much wider than they first imagined.

You can create
almost anything
you can think of.
